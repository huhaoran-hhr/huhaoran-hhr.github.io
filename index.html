About

I am a second year math PhD student at Northwestern University. My study interests lie in probability and mathematical physics, with a particular focus on the phase transitions, mixings, and universality in lattice models, as well as connections with spectral theory and analytic number theory.

Before Northwestern, I studied pure mathematics at the Courant Institute and at Shandong University.
